The device-master-volume fix was only partly integrated into the shipped LVA
baseline: the peripheral_api.py and __main__.py halves are present, the
entity.py half was not. Those three call sites set both mpv players to the
device volume percentage whenever Home Assistant sets a volume or an unmute
runs, so the user volume was applied twice - once by biscuit-dsp, which owns
it, and again by mpv.

mpv's volume curve is cubic, so the second application is far heavier than it
looks: measured on device, 50% is -18.06 dB and 25% is -36.12 dB. At a device
volume of 50 the assistant's own sounds therefore came out 28 dB down instead
of 10, and at 25 they were 56 dB down and effectively inaudible. It showed up
first on the mute and unmute earcons because those two files are already the
quietest in the set, at -30 and -34 dBFS RMS.
